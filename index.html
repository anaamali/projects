Demo · Real-World Data Configurator

How a typo stops turning into a wrong delivery.

Corvana delivers real-world data extracts to enterprise clients every month. The Configurator replaced hand-coded specs, spreadsheet schedules and side-channel releases with one reviewed, versioned and auditable path.

Problem

30+ manual steps across four teams. Specs hand-coded by engineers. 9 redeliveries in a 12-month period, at about $5k each.

Build

A self-service tool to configure, schedule and release every extract. Customer Success enters specs, a second person reviews them, the pipeline checks the output, and one click releases it.

Result

1 redelivery in the 12 months that followed, with more than $1.1M of ACV no longer exposed to a wrong delivery. About 10 manual steps cut from every delivery.

About the tool. A self-service tool that empowers the configuration of Corvana Real World Data extracts for enterprise clients. It ensures fast, accurate onboarding with auditable setup, driving scalability, trust and operational efficiency, and supports Corvana's strategic goal of becoming a foundational data provider for life science companies.

The Demo Build is a working replica built. All company and customer names are fictional. The extract location, QA checklist and the typo scenario are illustrative.
